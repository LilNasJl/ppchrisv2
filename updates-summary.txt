                    SYSTEM ENHANCEMENTS AND PAGE UPDATES
================================================================================

--- SPEED & PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATIONS (FASTER LOADING & QUERIES) ---

* Daily Time Record (DTR) Speed Boost
  - Attendance records are now retrieved and processed in bulk batches instead 
    of checking each day one by one. This noticeably speeds up opening and 
    refreshing attendance pages.

* Quicker Reports Generation
  - Optimized the search system for workforce reports (such as Tardiness / Late 
    Rankings, Absences, and Overtime). The database now instantly zeroes in 
    on the selected payroll period, making report previews and exports generate 
    much faster.

* Instant Summary Card Calculations (Designations, Branches, Departments)
  - The top summary cards calculate employee totals and role assignments 
    directly at the database level, ensuring pages load quickly without 
    lagging even as staff counts grow.

* Faster Employee Portal Loading (Profile, Leaves, and Loans)
  - Calculations for loan balances, remaining leave credits, and deductions are 
    now streamlined to load immediately without fetching unnecessary background 
    data, keeping pages responsive on both mobile and desktop.
